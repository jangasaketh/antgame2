    Five floors down inside a living burrow

    This is not a maze of corridors. It is a burrow — wide chambers hollowed out of the soil, humped with hills, joined by tunnels that narrow until you have to squeeze. Chambers look different for a reason: the brood rooms, the granary, the larder where the honeypot ants hang. Learn them, because the way down is not marked until you find it.
